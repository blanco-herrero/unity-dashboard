Interpretation and data notes

Timeliness is already reverse-coded as 6 minus the original mean. Reference lines use unweighted method medians. Faster methods and stronger evidence appear toward the upper right; median lines are relative references, not absolute thresholds.

Evidence ratings are conditional on method–objective relevance. Assessment counts are not necessarily independent respondent counts or statistical precision. Some estimates use very small samples.

Objective-participant timeliness can change due to respondent composition; this does not demonstrate that a method works faster for a particular objective.

Missing evidence can reflect no relevance selection or declining assessment due to lack of knowledge. The dataset does not identify the reason. Missing scores are not plotted and no values are silently substituted.

Within-objective scaling sets the largest bubble to the reference area for 100 assessments in the overview. Other areas retain their count proportions. Disable this scaling for common count scaling across views. The size slider adds a common multiplier. Zoom changes axis limits, not values.

The supplied overview includes the confirmed M14 correction: 33 evidence assessments and mean evidence 3.12. Original numbering is retained; M25 has no asterisk except for objective-specific missing evidence.

Exports include method IDs. Full method names are in tooltips, details and CSV data.
